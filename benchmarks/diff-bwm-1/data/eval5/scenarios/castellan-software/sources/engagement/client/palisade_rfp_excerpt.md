## 3.4 Digital Delivery Requirements

3.4.1 Palisade Power & Light ("the Company") is modernizing its ratings and asset data intake. Effective with this contract cycle, engineering deliverables consisting primarily of tabular or structured data shall be delivered in machine-readable form in addition to any narrative report format.

3.4.2 Awardee shall provide a secure client portal and a documented REST API for machine-readable delivery of facility ratings results no later than twelve (12) months after contract award.

3.4.3 The portal shall provide, at minimum: authenticated access for Company-designated users; deliverable download with version history; and notification of new or revised deliverables. The API shall provide, at minimum: retrieval of current facility ratings values by facility identifier; retrieval of rating methodology metadata (method, assumptions, effective date); and a change feed identifying ratings revised since a caller-supplied date.

3.4.4 Static document delivery (PDF or equivalent) may continue during the transition period defined in 3.4.2 but shall not satisfy the requirements of this section thereafter. Deliverables submitted solely as static documents after the transition period will be deemed nonconforming.

3.4.5 Failure to meet the requirements of this Section 3.4 within the period defined in 3.4.2 shall constitute grounds for termination for convenience under Section 9.2 and will be considered in any renewal determination.

## 3.5 Data Refresh and Compliance Evidence

3.5.1 Awardee shall refresh facility ratings data quarterly, with each refresh completed and available via the mechanisms of Section 3.4 within fifteen (15) business days of the close of the calendar quarter.

3.5.2 Corrections to previously delivered ratings values shall be delivered within five (5) business days of identification, flagged as corrections, and accompanied by a description of the affected records and the cause.

3.5.3 For deliverables supporting the Company's NERC FAC-008 facility ratings program, Awardee shall be capable of producing, on request, an audit-evidence export: the data lineage for any delivered rating value, including source data identification, calculation method and version, and reviewer approval records. Export shall be available within ten (10) business days of a Company request, in a format suitable for submission to the Company's compliance function.

3.5.4 Awardee shall retain records supporting delivered ratings values for the life of the contract plus six (6) years.

## 3.6 Information Security Requirements

3.6.1 Any hosted system used to store, process, or deliver Company data — including the portal and API described in Section 3.4 — shall be covered by a current SOC 2 Type II report or an equivalent independent attestation acceptable to the Company. Awardee shall furnish the report or attestation prior to system go-live and annually thereafter.

3.6.2 Company data shall be encrypted in transit and at rest. Access shall be role-based and limited to personnel with a need to know. Awardee shall notify the Company of any security incident affecting Company data within seventy-two (72) hours of discovery.

3.6.3 Company data classified as Critical Energy/Electric Infrastructure Information (CEII) shall be handled in accordance with the Company's CEII handling procedures, incorporated by reference as Exhibit F.

3.6.4 Subcontractors and hosted-service providers with access to Company data are subject to the requirements of this Section 3.6; Awardee remains responsible for their compliance.
