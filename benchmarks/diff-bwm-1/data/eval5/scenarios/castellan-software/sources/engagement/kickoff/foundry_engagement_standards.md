# Foundry Systems Advisory
## Engagement Deliverable Standards — v4.2 (2026-01)

**Owner:** Practice Standards Committee
**Applies to:** All client-facing deliverables (assessments, capability plans, roadmaps, memoranda) produced by Foundry engagement teams.
**Supersedes:** v4.1 (2024-09)

### Purpose

Foundry's reputation rests on deliverables a client can audit. Every figure traceable, every recommendation properly gated, every document consistent with the last one we shipped. These standards are mandatory. Deliverables that do not conform will be returned by the reviewing partner without substantive review.

### Standards

**1. Currency formatting.** All dollar amounts are expressed in USD with the '$' symbol and comma thousands separators. Amounts under $1,000,000 are written in full digits (e.g., $58,800 — never '$59K'). Amounts of $1,000,000 and above may use the $X.XM form (e.g., $2.8M). Recurring costs must always state the period ('/yr' or '/mo'). Never mix forms within a single table.

**2. Spelling.** US English spelling throughout: 'analyze', 'organization', 'modeling', 'license' (noun and verb). Engagement teams staffed from the London office take particular note.

**3. Source attribution.** Every numeric claim in a deliverable carries an inline source attribution in the form '(source: <document name>)'. A number without a named source is an opinion, and Foundry does not bill for opinions. Where a figure is derived rather than quoted, attribute the input documents and show the arithmetic.

**4. Gate language.** No vendor or product may be named as a final selection before the client-approved Phase 1 validation gate. Any candidate product named in a deliverable must be accompanied by the exact phrase 'subject to Phase 1 validation'. The phrases 'recommendation of record' and 'final vendor selection' must not appear anywhere in an engagement deliverable prior to the gate — in any context, including direct quotation or paraphrase of this standard; cite the standard by number instead. This standard exists because of the Meridian litigation in 2021; it is not negotiable.

**5. Sources Consulted section.** Every prose deliverable closes with a 'Sources Consulted' section listing the documents relied upon, by name. If a document in the engagement room was reviewed and deliberately excluded as unreliable or out of scope, best practice is to say so.

**6. Client naming.** Refer to the client by its agreed engagement short name after first full mention. Do not use client initialisms unless the engagement addendum explicitly approves one; initialisms collide across Foundry's client base and have caused conflicts-desk incidents.

**7. Dates.** All dates in deliverables use the YYYY-MM-DD format. Month names may be used in narrative prose ('the March incident') but any date carrying contractual or scheduling weight is written 2026-03-30.

**8. Headings.** Deliverable headings use sentence case, not title case. Numbered headings for any document over five pages.

**9. Confidentiality banner.** All deliverables carry the standard footer: 'Prepared by Foundry Systems Advisory for [client]. Confidential — not for distribution.'

### Engagement addendum — Castellan Grid Consulting (issued 2026-06-05, engagement lead)

Per Standard 6: refer to the client as 'Castellan' after the first full mention of Castellan Grid Consulting. Never use the initialism 'CGC', which collides with another Foundry client. Note that the client's own file servers and internal documents use the CGC prefix; quote such artifacts verbatim where needed (e.g., server paths), but do not adopt the initialism in Foundry prose.

### Review and enforcement

The engagement lead is accountable for conformance. The reviewing partner spot-checks Standards 1, 3, and 4 on every deliverable; a Standard 4 violation is escalated to the practice head the same day. Questions on interpretation go to the Practice Standards Committee inbox, not to Slack.
