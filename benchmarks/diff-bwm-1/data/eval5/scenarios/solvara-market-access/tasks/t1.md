# Task 1: Market Access Strategy & Prior-Authorization Workflow

## Objective

Design (1) the US market access strategy and (2) the end-to-end prior-authorization workflow for the launch of Solvara (velankibart), a subcutaneous self-administered biologic for GPP, ahead of the expected Q4 2026 PDUFA approval. Ground every quantitative claim in the source pool, resolve the conflicts between the launch deck / junior memo and the underlying evidence, and respect the legal and statutory overlays (benefit-type routing, copay eligibility, PA-turnaround statutes).

## Requirements

- Segment the US payer landscape (commercial, Medicare Part D, Medicaid, uninsured) with evidence-based shares, and specify the correct benefit-type routing for each — Solvara is a pharmacy-benefit product (Medicare via Part D), not a Part B buy-and-bill product.
- Size the opportunity: report the diagnosed and the addressable GPP population, deriving the addressable figure by triangulating the epidemiology report, the claims extract, and the advisory board rather than adopting the launch deck's headline.
- Design an end-to-end prior-authorization workflow covering intake, benefits verification (including ICD-10 L40.1 confirmation), PA preparation/submission, determination, denial and multi-level appeals, specialty-pharmacy fulfillment, bridge/PAP routing, and adherence follow-up, with realistic SLAs that honor state PA-turnaround statutes.
- Design patient support (hub services, bridge program, copay program, PAP) that respects the legal constraint that copay assistance is for commercially-insured patients only and that government-insured patients are routed to an independent charitable foundation or the PAP.
- Recommend the specialty-pharmacy distribution model and network breadth (how many pharmacies, and why), grounded in the network-options analysis rather than the deck's open-distribution assumption.
- State the pricing economics using the source-grounded figures, not the launch deck's optimistic numbers: expected first-pass PA approval, Solvara's planned WAC (list price), and blended gross-to-net erosion. In `market_access_plan.md`, flag each place your analysis overrides the deck or the junior memo and say why.
- Follow the Copeland Engagement Standards (`sources/shared/reference/copeland_engagement_standards.md`) for units, spelling, dates, inline source citation, controlled vocabularies, the no-recommendation-of-record-before-gate rule, and the copay compliance line, in both deliverables.
- Alongside the two deliverables, submit `claims.json` containing a `key_figures` object with these exact keys: `us_addressable_patients` (addressable US GPP patients), `blended_first_pass_pa_approval_pct` (share-weighted first-pass PA approval across insured lives, as a fraction 0-1), `blended_gross_to_net_pct` (blended gross-to-net erosion as a fraction 0-1), `annual_wac_usd` (Solvara's planned WAC in USD per patient per year).

## Deliverables

**Filenames:**

1. `market_access_plan.md` — the US market access strategy (prose).
2. `pa_workflow.yaml` — the machine-readable prior-authorization workflow spec.
3. `claims.json` — the headline key figures.

### `pa_workflow.yaml` — required schema

A machine-readable workflow spec with these top-level keys:

1. `product`: an object naming the product and its benefit routing (`brand`, `generic`, `route_of_administration`, `benefit_type`, `medicare_pathway`).
2. `payer_segments`: a list of objects, one per segment (commercial, medicare, medicaid, uninsured), each with fields `segment`, `share_pct`, `benefit_design`, `pa_required`, `expected_first_pass_approval_pct`, `turnaround_days_standard`, `turnaround_days_expedited`.
3. `workflow_steps`: a list of at least 12 objects, each with `step_id`, `name`, `actor`, `system`, `inputs`, `outputs`, `sla_business_days`, `failure_paths` — covering intake, benefits verification, PA preparation, PA submission, determination, denial handling, appeal levels, specialty-pharmacy fulfillment, bridge/PAP routing, nursing/injection training, and adherence follow-up.
4. `appeals`: a list of at least 3 level objects (e.g. payer reconsideration/internal appeal, peer-to-peer, external/independent review or Part D redetermination ladder), each with `level`, `name`, `actor`, `sla`.
5. `patient_support`: an object with `hub_services` (list), `bridge_program` (object incl. `duration_days` and `eligibility`), and `copay_program` (object incl. `annual_max_usd`, `eligible_population`, and the legal constraint that federal healthcare program beneficiaries are not eligible) — plus a pap/foundation route for government-insured and uninsured patients.
6. `kpis`: a list of at least 5 objects, each with `kpi`, `target`, `rationale`.

Use the controlled vocabularies from the engagement standards: `benefit_design`/`benefit_type` one of 'pharmacy benefit' or 'medical benefit'; PA outcomes 'approved' | 'denied' | 'pended'.

### `claims.json` — required schema

```json
{"key_figures": {"us_addressable_patients": <number>, "blended_first_pass_pa_approval_pct": <fraction 0-1>, "blended_gross_to_net_pct": <fraction 0-1>, "annual_wac_usd": <USD per patient per year>}}
```
