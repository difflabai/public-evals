## About Veloxa

Veloxa Therapeutics, Inc. is a US specialty pharmaceutical company headquartered in Cambridge, Massachusetts, with commercial and medical-affairs operations in Parsippany, New Jersey. Founded in 2011, Veloxa develops and commercializes therapies for serious immune-mediated and dermatologic conditions with high unmet need. The company reported approximately $900 million in net revenue for fiscal year 2025, the substantial majority of which derives from Veloxa's in-licensed and co-promoted immunology portfolio.

For most of its history Veloxa has commercialized its products through partnership structures — co-promotion arrangements, licensed distribution, and reliance on partner-operated patient-support and reimbursement infrastructure. Solvara (velankibart) represents a strategic inflection point: it is the first product Veloxa owns outright and intends to launch and support with its own commercial organization, from a therapy the company discovered and developed through its internal research programs. That distinction matters operationally, because it means Veloxa must stand up market-access, distribution, and patient-support capabilities it has historically rented rather than built.

Veloxa employs approximately 1,400 people across research, development, medical affairs, commercial, and corporate functions. The company is publicly traded (NASDAQ) and is followed by specialty-pharma analysts principally for its immunology franchise and for the Solvara launch, which is widely viewed as the most consequential catalyst in the company's pipeline.

## Solvara at a glance

Solvara (velankibart) is Veloxa's lead proprietary asset and its first wholly owned commercial product. Key facts:

- **Molecule:** velankibart, a biologic (monoclonal antibody) targeting the interleukin-36 (IL-36) signaling pathway implicated in the pathogenesis of generalized pustular psoriasis (GPP).
- **Indication (pending approval):** maintenance treatment of generalized pustular psoriasis (GPP), a rare, severe, and potentially life-threatening dermatologic condition characterized by recurrent flares of widespread sterile pustules.
- **Route and administration:** Solvara is a **subcutaneous biologic self-administered by the patient at home** via prefilled syringe / autoinjector on a maintenance dosing schedule. It is not an in-office infusion product.
- **Regulatory status:** the Biologics License Application is under FDA review; the company expects a PDUFA action date in the **fourth quarter of 2026**. Launch planning is underway as of June 2026 on the assumption of an on-time approval.
- **Setting:** Solvara is positioned for the maintenance setting. Acute GPP flares — which can require inpatient management — are handled under separate acute-care pathways and are outside Solvara's maintenance indication.

Because GPP is a rare disease, the eligible commercial population is small and highly specialized, concentrated among dermatologists and a limited number of academic and referral centers. This shapes nearly every downstream decision: how the product is distributed, how prior authorization is managed, and how patients are supported financially and clinically.

## The Solvara launch organization

Ownership of the Solvara launch sits with the commercial organization, coordinated through a cross-functional launch team. Key leaders relevant to market access and patient support:

- **Marcus Feld — Vice President, Market Access.** Executive sponsor for payer strategy, distribution, and the patient-support model. Marcus owns the relationship with Copeland Access Partners and is accountable for the access plan the launch depends on.
- **Renu Iyer — Director, Launch Excellence.** Owns launch-readiness planning, the cross-functional milestone tracker, and the internal launch-strategy deck. Renu coordinates the workstreams but is not the domain authority on any single access figure.
- **Sofia Marchetti — Senior Director, Pricing & Contracting.** Owns list-price strategy, gross-to-net planning, payer contracting, and government-pricing compliance (Medicaid, 340B).
- **Daniel Roth — Associate General Counsel, Healthcare Compliance.** Advises on fraud-and-abuse law, patient-affordability program design, promotional compliance, and the legal guardrails around copay and foundation support.
- **Dr. Alan Priest — Vice President, Medical Affairs.** Owns scientific and medical strategy, advisory boards, and evidence generation (dosing, real-world data, KOL engagement).

The launch team meets weekly and reports to the Solvara Launch Steering Committee, chaired by the Chief Commercial Officer.

## Why Veloxa engaged an outside access firm

Veloxa has deep immunology commercial experience but **no in-house specialty-drug market-access or patient-support infrastructure of its own** — no established payer-policy analytics team, no owned hub-services operation, no specialty-pharmacy network, and no prior-authorization workflow built for a self-administered specialty biologic. Historically these functions were provided by co-promotion partners.

Rather than assemble that machinery from scratch on a compressed launch timeline, Veloxa engaged Copeland Access Partners, a US market-access consultancy, to design (a) the US market-access strategy for Solvara and (b) the end-to-end prior-authorization workflow, and to recommend the distribution and patient-support architecture. The engagement is sponsored by Marcus Feld and is expected to produce launch-ready deliverables ahead of the anticipated Q4 2026 approval. Nothing in the access design is final until it clears Veloxa legal review and the pricing committee.
