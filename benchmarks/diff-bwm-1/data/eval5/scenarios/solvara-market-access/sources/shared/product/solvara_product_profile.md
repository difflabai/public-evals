# Solvara (velankibart) — Product Profile

*Veloxa Therapeutics, Inc. — Medical Affairs / Commercial. Version 2.1. Last updated: 2026-05-28. Authoritative internal product reference. Not for promotional use.*

---

## 1. Summary

Solvara (velankibart) is an investigational, subcutaneously administered biologic being developed by Veloxa Therapeutics for the maintenance treatment of generalized pustular psoriasis (GPP). The Biologics License Application is under FDA review with an anticipated PDUFA action date in the fourth quarter of 2026. This profile is the authoritative internal reference for Solvara's indication, mechanism, route of administration, benefit adjudication pathway, and planned pricing intent. Where other planning artifacts (launch decks, draft memos) conflict with this profile on route of administration or benefit type, this profile governs.

## 2. Indication (pending approval)

Solvara is being developed for the **maintenance treatment of generalized pustular psoriasis (GPP)** in adults. GPP is a rare, severe, and potentially life-threatening neutrophilic dermatosis distinct from the more common plaque psoriasis (psoriasis vulgaris). It is characterized by recurrent flares of widespread sterile pustules on erythematous skin, frequently accompanied by systemic inflammation, fever, and malaise, and can require hospitalization.

The Solvara program distinguishes two clinical settings:

- **Acute flare management** — the treatment of an active, acute pustular flare, which may require inpatient or urgent care. Acute flare therapy is managed under separate acute-care pathways and is **not** the indication under review for Solvara.
- **Maintenance treatment** — ongoing therapy in the inter-flare period to reduce the frequency and severity of future flares and to maintain disease control. This is Solvara's intended setting.

Correct disease coding is material to coverage. GPP codes to **ICD-10-CM L40.1**. Miscoding to L40.0 (psoriasis vulgaris) is a recognized cause of coverage friction and prior-authorization denial; see the ICD-10 coding reference for detail.

## 3. Mechanism of action

Velankibart is a humanized monoclonal antibody that modulates the **interleukin-36 (IL-36) signaling pathway**. IL-36 cytokine signaling (IL-36α, IL-36β, IL-36γ acting through the IL-36 receptor) is a central driver of the neutrophilic inflammation that characterizes GPP; loss-of-function variants in the IL-36 receptor antagonist (IL36RN) are associated with the disease. By blocking IL-36 receptor–mediated signaling, velankibart is designed to interrupt the inflammatory amplification loop that produces pustular flares, supporting sustained maintenance control.

The IL-36 mechanism is disease-specific to GPP and its pustular phenotype and is distinct from the IL-17 and IL-23 pathways targeted by biologics used for plaque psoriasis. This mechanistic specificity underpins Solvara's positioning as a targeted GPP maintenance therapy rather than a general psoriasis agent.

## 4. Route of administration and benefit pathway

Route of administration determines how Solvara is adjudicated and paid, and it is the single most consequential fact in this profile for access design.

> **Solvara is a subcutaneous biologic self-administered by the patient at home; it adjudicates on the pharmacy benefit, and Medicare coverage is provided under Part D.**

There is no in-office or facility administration event to purchase and bill. Because the patient (or caregiver) administers the injection at home, Solvara is dispensed through the pharmacy channel — specifically through specialty pharmacies — and claims adjudicate on the **pharmacy benefit**, not the medical benefit. For Medicare beneficiaries, coverage runs through **Part D** (the outpatient prescription-drug benefit) via the plan's coverage-determination process, **not** through Part B buy-and-bill under a physician-administered (J-code) reimbursement model. Any access design that routes Solvara as a buy-and-bill / medical-benefit product is inconsistent with the product's route of administration and site of care.

## 5. Dosing and administration

- **Formulation:** liquid biologic supplied in a single-dose prefilled syringe and single-dose prefilled autoinjector.
- **Administration:** subcutaneous injection, self-administered by the patient or caregiver at home following training.
- **Regimen (planned):** a maintenance schedule consisting of an initial loading phase followed by maintenance dosing at a fixed multi-week interval. Precise dosing is per the final approved label; planning assumes a maintenance dosing cadence consistent with a self-injected specialty biologic.
- **Training and support:** because patients self-inject, injection training (nurse-delivered, in person or via telehealth), adherence support, and cold-chain handling from the specialty pharmacy to the patient's home are integral to the delivery model.
- **Storage:** refrigerated (cold chain); requires temperature-controlled distribution and at-home refrigerated storage with defined excursion limits.

## 6. Pricing intent

Solvara's **planned WAC of $150,000 per patient per year** (list / wholesale acquisition cost) reflects its positioning as a rare-disease specialty biologic addressing a severe, potentially life-threatening condition with limited targeted treatment options. This is the planned list price; net realization after rebates, statutory Medicaid rebates, 340B, and patient-affordability support is materially lower and is addressed in the pricing and contracting memo, which is authoritative for gross-to-net. The $150,000 per-patient-per-year WAC is Solvara's price and should not be conflated with the list prices of analog or reference biologics used as coverage proxies.

## 7. Safety and monitoring (planning summary)

As an IL-36 pathway–modulating biologic, velankibart's anticipated safety considerations include injection-site reactions, hypersensitivity, and infection risk consistent with the immunomodulatory class; final safety labeling is per the approved USPI. Because patients self-administer at home, the delivery model builds in nurse-led injection training, a first-injection support touchpoint, and adherence follow-up so that safety counseling and proper technique are reinforced outside the clinic. Cold-chain integrity from the specialty pharmacy to the patient's refrigerator is part of product quality and must be maintained within defined temperature-excursion limits; the patient-support model includes handling guidance and replacement logistics for compromised shipments.

## 8. Coding note

For benefits verification and prior authorization, confirm the diagnosis is coded to **ICD-10-CM L40.1 (generalized pustular psoriasis)**. Do not accept L40.0 (psoriasis vulgaris), which describes a different disease and will not support a GPP-specific coverage determination. Documentation should include the L40.1 diagnosis, relevant chart notes describing the pustular presentation, and prior therapy history.
