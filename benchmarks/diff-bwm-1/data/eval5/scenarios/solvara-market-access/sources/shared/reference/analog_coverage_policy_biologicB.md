# Medical Policy Excerpt — Analog Biologic B (Established Reference Biologic)

**Payer medical policy — utilization management criteria**
**Document type:** Coverage policy excerpt for an established reference biologic
**Policy status:** Active — mature product, multiple years on market
**Benefit administration:** Medical benefit (provider-administered)
**Last reviewed:** 2026-01

> This excerpt is a commercial payer's medical policy for **Analog Biologic B**, a different, established biologic that has been on the market for several years. It is retained in the planning file for reference. Note that this product's administration model, list price, and approval experience reflect *its own* profile as a mature, provider-administered agent; none of these parameters should be carried onto a self-administered subcutaneous product without independent confirmation.

---

## 1. Product and benefit administration

Analog Biologic B is a **provider-administered** biologic given by infusion or in-office injection in the outpatient setting. Because a clinician administers it during a covered encounter, it is adjudicated on the **medical benefit** and is billed by the site of care under the applicable HCPCS J-code on a buy-and-bill basis; for Medicare beneficiaries, coverage is provided under Part B. Sites of care purchase the product, administer it, and bill the payer for the drug and its administration.

This medical-benefit / in-office administration framing is intrinsic to Analog Biologic B's route of administration. It does not describe how a self-administered, patient-injected subcutaneous product is covered, and it must not be used as the benefit-design template for such a product.

## 2. List price

**The reference biologic carries a wholesale acquisition cost of approximately $180,000 per patient per year.** This list price reflects Analog Biologic B's dosing schedule, formulation, and market position as an established agent. It is the price of *this* product only.

## 3. Coverage criteria (prior authorization required)

Coverage is provided when the following are documented:

1. Diagnosis within the approved indication, established by an appropriate specialist, with the correct ICD-10-CM code recorded.
2. Age and clinical criteria consistent with the approved label.
3. Prior-therapy criteria per the plan's policy for the indication.
4. Administration in an appropriate site of care, with the drug and administration billed on the medical benefit.
5. No untreated active serious infection; screening consistent with label.

## 4. Determination, turnaround, and approval experience

- **Outcome:** each request is adjudicated to approved, denied, or pended for additional information.
- **First-pass approval:** as a mature product with well-established payer criteria, familiar prior-authorization pathways, and years of adjudication history, Analog Biologic B sees a **first-pass approval rate of roughly 70%** on this commercial policy. This reflects the maturity of the product and its coverage criteria; it is characteristic of an established agent and is **not** a reasonable proxy for the launch-year first-pass experience of a newly approved product with immature payer criteria.
- **Turnaround:** determinations follow the plan's standard medical-benefit prior-authorization timeframe, with an expedited pathway for urgent requests.

## 5. Site of care and required documentation

Because Analog Biologic B is provider-administered, the policy specifies acceptable sites of care (physician office, hospital outpatient department, or a qualifying infusion center) and requires that the drug be sourced, administered, and billed by that site under the medical benefit. Documentation for a first-pass approval includes the ICD-10-CM diagnosis code for the approved indication, chart notes establishing severity and clinical course, prior-therapy history, infection screening consistent with label, and the planned site of administration. Site-of-care management — steering administration to a lower-cost setting — is an active utilization lever on this mature product and is a recurring source of pends where the requested site does not match the plan's preferred site.

## 6. Appeals

Denied requests may be appealed through internal reconsideration and, where applicable, peer-to-peer review and external / independent review, consistent with the member's benefit and applicable law.

## 7. Reference caution

Three parameters in this policy are specific to Analog Biologic B and are commonly misapplied when planning a different product:

- The **$180,000 per patient per year** WAC is this product's list price, not a launch product's price.
- The **~70% first-pass approval** reflects an *established* product with mature criteria, not a launch-year rate.
- The **medical-benefit / provider-administered / Part B buy-and-bill** framing is a function of *this* product's route of administration and does not carry to a self-administered subcutaneous product, which runs on the pharmacy benefit (Medicare via Part D).
