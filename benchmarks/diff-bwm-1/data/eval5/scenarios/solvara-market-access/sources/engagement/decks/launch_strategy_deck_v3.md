## Slide 1 — Solvara: a category-defining launch

**Speaker notes:**
- Solvara (velankibart) is Veloxa's first wholly owned launch and our most important catalyst.
- First-in-disease targeted maintenance therapy for generalized pustular psoriasis (GPP), a rare and potentially life-threatening condition with no purpose-built maintenance option today.
- PDUFA expected Q4 2026. We launch into a rare, severe, underserved market with high urgency of need and a receptive prescriber base concentrated in dermatology and referral centers.
- This is Veloxa's first wholly owned launch, so we are also standing up access, distribution, and patient-support capabilities for the first time.
- Our ambition: fast, broad access from day one. This deck lays out the commercial and access assumptions underpinning the plan, all of which the access workstream is validating.

## Slide 2 — The opportunity is bigger than the "rare disease" label suggests

**Speaker notes:**
- GPP is called rare, but the treatable population is larger than the classic prevalence estimates imply once you account for undiagnosed and under-treated patients who will surface as awareness rises post-launch.
- **Total addressable US GPP population of up to 12,000 patients represents the launch opportunity.**
- Even at conservative penetration, this supports a franchise-scale revenue trajectory.
- Marketing will build demand-generation around driving diagnosis and referral, expanding the funnel over the first three years.

## Slide 3 — A commercially favorable payer landscape

**Speaker notes:**
- GPP skews toward working-age adults, which is good news for our payer mix and net pricing.
- **We assume a commercially-favorable payer mix of roughly 70% commercial, with government payers a minority of demand.**
- Commercial concentration supports our net-price and copay-program economics and limits Medicaid drag.
- Government channels (Medicare, Medicaid) exist but are a smaller slice; we will manage them but they do not drive the model.

## Slide 4 — Access will be fast and high-yield

**Speaker notes:**
- We are designing a best-in-class access experience: simple intake, fast benefits verification, rapid PA turnaround.
- **We model an 80% first-pass prior-authorization approval rate at launch.**
- Strong clinical story (severe, life-threatening disease; first targeted maintenance option) supports high approval.
- Field reimbursement and the hub will drive first-pass yield up over the launch curve; abandonment will be low.

## Slide 5 — Distribution: go broad for access

**Speaker notes:**
- To maximize patient access and minimize friction, we favor breadth of distribution.
- **Launch through open distribution across 15 specialty pharmacies to maximize access.**
- More pharmacies = more prescriber choice, fewer channel bottlenecks, faster fills.
- Trade & distribution to finalize the pharmacy roster; the working assumption is broad availability.

## Slide 6 — Patient support: remove every barrier

**Speaker notes:**
- Affordability and continuity of therapy are our biggest abandonment risks in a high-cost specialty launch.
- **A generous 90-day bridge program will keep patients on therapy while PAs are pending.**
- Robust copay support to drive commercial patients to $0 out of pocket.
- Nurse injection training and adherence outreach from the hub.
- Goal: no eligible patient leaves the funnel for financial or administrative reasons.

## Slide 7 — Pricing and net realization

**Speaker notes:**
- Planned WAC positions Solvara appropriately as a rare-disease specialty biologic.
- On the commercially favorable mix above, **we assume roughly 30% gross-to-net erosion**, supporting strong net realization per patient.
- Contracting strategy to secure preferred formulary access on major commercial and Part D plans.
- *(Pricing & Contracting to confirm; Sofia's workstream is refining the gross-to-net build.)*

## Slide 8 — Competitive context

**Speaker notes:**
- GPP has historically been managed off-label with conventional systemics and biologics developed for plaque psoriasis; there has been no targeted maintenance option purpose-built for the pustular phenotype.
- Solvara enters as a first-in-disease targeted maintenance therapy, which we believe supports premium positioning and strong payer receptivity on clinical grounds.
- Reference biologics used in the category offer a directional read on coverage behavior, but Solvara's disease-specific profile should command better first-pass performance than the older analogs.
- Positioning line: "the first maintenance therapy designed for GPP" (pending MLR).

## Slide 9 — Launch KPIs (working targets)

**Speaker notes:**
- Time-to-first-fill: aggressive target from prescription to first shipment; the hub and field reimbursement drive the curve.
- First-pass PA approval: 80% (see Slide 4).
- Patient abandonment: keep in single digits via bridge + copay.
- Breadth of pharmacy availability: 15 SPs live at launch.
- Appeal overturn rate: high, on the strength of the clinical story.
- Adherence / persistence: strong PDC via nurse training and refill outreach.
- Diagnosed-patient funnel growth: expand toward the 12,000 addressable ceiling over three years.

## Slide 10 — Field reimbursement model

**Speaker notes:**
- Field reimbursement managers (FRMs) support offices with benefits verification, PA navigation, and appeals.
- FRMs coordinate with the hub and specialty pharmacies to keep patients moving through the funnel.
- Emphasis on speed: benefits verification within 24 hours, PA submission same day where possible.
- Escalation paths for pended and denied cases, with peer-to-peer facilitation.
- Territory design to concentrate coverage on the highest-volume dermatology and referral centers.

## Slide 11 — Timeline and readiness

**Speaker notes:**
- Q3 2026: finalize distribution roster, hub build, payer engagement, and field reimbursement hiring.
- Q4 2026: anticipated PDUFA; launch readiness gate; first-fill readiness across the specialty-pharmacy network.
- Cross-functional readiness tracked in the launch-readiness tracker, with weekly RAG review at the launch stand-up.
- Access workstream (Copeland) to validate payer, PA, and pricing assumptions and return the "real numbers" ahead of the gate.
- Dependencies: hub SOW, specialty-pharmacy contracting, and legal sign-off on affordability programs are on the critical path; slippage on any of them moves first fill.
- Contingency: if PDUFA shifts, the whole readiness timeline moves with it and the demand forecast is rebased.

## Slide 12 — Summary of planning assumptions

**Speaker notes:**
- Addressable population: up to 12,000.
- Payer mix: ~70% commercial.
- First-pass PA approval: 80%.
- Distribution: open, 15 specialty pharmacies.
- Bridge: 90 days.
- Gross-to-net: ~30% erosion.
- **These are planning assumptions for alignment, not validated figures.** The access workstream will confirm, correct, or replace each of these before the readiness gate.
