## Standing risks / watch items

- **Access assumptions under validation.** Several planning figures in the strategy deck (addressable population, payer mix, first-pass approval, distribution breadth, bridge length) are being validated by the Copeland access workstream and may change before the gate. Forecasting (#23) is holding on the validated addressable number.
- **Hub / distribution interdependency.** Workstreams 4, 5, 6, 21, and 22 all depend on the network and hub-parameter decisions; slippage there cascades.
- **Legal sign-offs.** Copay/foundation (#11), government pricing (#10), and PAP (#12) require compliance sign-off before build-out.
- **Timeline sensitivity.** All dates assume an on-time Q4 2026 PDUFA. A shift moves the gate (#24) and downstream dates.

*This tracker is an internal PMO artifact for coordination only. It does not carry approved access, pricing, or forecasting figures; refer to the owning workstreams' deliverables for those.*
