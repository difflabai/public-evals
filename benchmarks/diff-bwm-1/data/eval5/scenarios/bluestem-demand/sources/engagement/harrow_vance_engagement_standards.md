### 1. Purpose

This memorandum sets the deliverable standards for the Bluestem Foodservice engagement. These are not suggestions. Every document, exhibit, model output, and readout page delivered to the client must conform. Team members are responsible for self-checking before internal review; the engagement manager will reject non-conforming drafts without further comment.

### 2. Writing and formatting standards

The following firm standards apply to all client deliverables on this engagement:

1. Dollar figures in client deliverables are written in USD with '$' — millions to one decimal as $12.4M, thousands as $410K. Never 'USD 12,400,000' or bare '12.4 million'.

2. Fiscal years are written as FY2025 (no space, four digits). Never 'FY 25' or '2025FY'.

3. Margin levels are stated in percent to one decimal (e.g., 19.8%); margin CHANGES are stated in basis points ('bps').

4. Banned phrases in client deliverables: 'synergy'/'synergies', 'world-class', 'best-in-class' (H&V writing standard; also a stated pet peeve of the client CEO).

5. Forward-looking revenue and margin impacts must be expressed as ranges (low-high bands), never single-point forecasts.

6. Every quantified initiative must name the client source document(s) supporting its sizing (document-name attribution, e.g. 'per financials_by_segment_fy23_fy25.csv').

7. US spelling throughout.

A note on item 4: Mrs. Weyandt-Ross has told two prior advisors that the first appearance of the word "synergies" in a deck is where she stops reading. Do not test her.

A note on item 5: single-point forecasts create false precision and invite anchoring in board discussion. State the low case, state the high case, and state what drives the spread. If you cannot construct a band, you have not finished the analysis.

A note on item 6: this client's internal documents contain figures that disagree with one another. Attribution is how we stay honest about which figure we used and why. An initiative sized "per management estimate" will be sent back.

### 3. Scope guardrails

The following boundaries were set in the CEO's engagement letter and confirmed at kickoff. They are binding:

- **Organic growth only, this phase.** The engagement addresses demand growth from the business Bluestem already operates. We are not evaluating acquisitions, and deliverables must not include M&A recommendations. Inbound broker material (there has already been some) is to be logged and set aside, not analyzed.

- **Recommendations must respect the client's financial envelope:** approximately $6M in annual capital expenditure capacity, a $25M revolver, and a maximum 3.0x net leverage covenant. Any initiative whose funding plan breaches that envelope is out of scope regardless of its return profile.

- **Recommendations must respect stated operational constraints** (distribution center capacity, fleet, and driver availability) as documented by the client's VP Operations. Do not size volume into a building that cannot rack it.

### 4. Evidence standards

Where internal client figures conflict — and on this engagement they do — deliverables must identify the conflict, name the authoritative source used to resolve it, and use that figure consistently thereafter. Do not average conflicting figures. Do not present both without adjudicating.

Survey data, benchmark studies, and third-party reports must be cited with cohort definitions checked. A benchmark quoted without confirming the comparison cohort is a defect, not a citation.

### 5. Review protocol

All quantitative exhibits require a second-person tie-out to source before client release. The engagement manager holds the tie-out log. Standard H&V document control, confidentiality, and retention policies apply.

Questions on any of the above come to me directly.
