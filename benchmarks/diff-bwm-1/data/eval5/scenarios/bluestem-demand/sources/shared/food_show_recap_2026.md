What a show!! Two days, one sold-out exhibit hall, and more energy than we've seen at a Bluestem event in years. ## The headline

Let me say it loud for the people in the back: **the show generated $3.2M in qualified pipeline**. That's the sum of qualified lead sheets collected across all 89 booths — customers who sat down with a vendor or a Bluestem rep, talked through a product or program, and indicated purchase intent with an estimated annual value. Leads are self-reported at the booth, and our vendor partners were fantastic about working the sheets this year. That's real demand walking around our show floor with a badge on.

Every lead sheet has been entered and routed to the owning rep as of Friday. Reps: your district managers have your follow-up lists. Strike while the brisket is hot!

## What worked

- **The Private Brands pavilion.** Renee's team ran cuttings all day both days. Prairie Table disposables samples ran out by 2 PM on day one (a good problem!).
- **Customer stories on the main stage.** Rosa's Cantina and USD 612 Larkin both spoke about their Bluestem partnership. You can't buy that kind of credibility. (We did buy them lunch.)
- **Vendor co-op participation** covered 62% of show costs versus 55% last year — meaning more show for the same Bluestem dollar.
