# Bluestem Foodservice Distributors, Inc.

## 2025 Sales Compensation Plan

**Document owner:** Human Resources / Sales Operations
**Effective date:** 2025-01-01
**Supersedes:** 2023 Sales Compensation Plan (as amended)
**Approval:** VP Sales; VP Human Resources; CFO
**Plan year:** January 1, 2025 – December 31, 2025 (carried forward for 2026 pending revision)

---

### 1. Purpose and Eligibility

1.1. This plan defines the compensation structure for Bluestem sales personnel. Participation is a condition of employment in an eligible role and does not constitute a contract of employment.

1.2. Eligible roles: Street Sales Representative, Senior Street Sales Representative, District Sales Manager, Territory Development Representative, National Accounts Manager (bonus provisions only; see Section 6).

1.3. The Company reserves the right to amend, suspend, or terminate this plan at any time with thirty (30) days' written notice. Interpretation of plan provisions rests with Sales Operations, with final authority residing with the CFO.

### 2. Definitions

2.1. **Gross Profit (GP):** invoiced sales less landed cost of goods, net of returns, allowances, and rebate accruals, as calculated by the Company's ERP at month close. GP is the sole commissionable basis under this plan.

2.2. **Book of Business:** the set of accounts assigned to a representative in the CRM as of the first day of the commission month.

2.3. **House Account:** an account designated by Sales Operations as serviced primarily through centralized administration rather than an assigned representative, or an order class designated for house-account processing under Section 4.

2.4. **Bluestem Direct:** the Company's customer-facing online ordering portal.

### 3. Commission Structure

3.1. **Street commission rate.** Street Sales Representatives and Senior Street Sales Representatives earn commission equal to **22% of gross profit dollars** generated by their assigned book of business, calculated and paid monthly, one month in arrears.

3.2. **Splits and transfers.** Where two representatives share an account by written agreement approved by the District Sales Manager, GP is split per the filed agreement. Accounts transferred mid-month commission to the representative of record at month open.

3.3. **New account incentive.** Accounts opened by a representative (first invoice) earn a 4-point commission uplift (26% of GP) for the first six invoiced months, reverting to the standard rate in month seven.

3.4. **House account rate.** GP generated on house accounts, and on order classes processed under house-account administration, is commissioned at **11% of gross profit dollars** to the representative of record, reflecting the reduced selling and service burden associated with centrally administered volume.

3.5. **Chargebacks.** Commissions on invoices unpaid at 90 days are reversed and re-credited upon collection.

### 4. Order Administration and Commission Classification

4.1. Orders entered by a representative via the order management system are classified as rep-entered and commission at the applicable Section 3.1 rate.

4.2. Orders phoned, faxed, or emailed to Customer Service by the customer and keyed by CSR staff are classified as rep-entered for commission purposes where an account assignment exists.

4.3. Orders placed through the Bluestem Direct portal are processed under house-account administration.

4.4. EDI orders from contract and chain accounts are processed under the National Accounts program (Section 6) and are not commissionable under Section 3.

### 5. Quarterly Volume Bonus

5.1. Representatives earn a quarterly bonus based on total invoiced cases against their assigned quota:

| Tier | Attainment vs. quarterly case quota | Bonus |
|------|-------------------------------------|-------|
| 1 | 95.0% – 99.9% | $750 |
| 2 | 100.0% – 104.9% | $1,750 |
| 3 | 105.0% – 109.9% | $3,000 |
| 4 | 110.0% and above | $4,500 |

5.2. Quotas are set annually by Sales Operations with District Sales Manager input and adjusted only for documented account transfers.

5.3. Bonus eligibility requires employment in good standing on the last day of the quarter.

### 6. National Accounts (Regional Chain) Program

6.1. Regional chain and contract accounts are managed by the salaried National Accounts team. National Accounts Managers are not commissioned under Section 3.

6.2. National Accounts Managers participate in an annual bonus plan targeted at 20% of base salary, weighted 60% on contract revenue retention and growth and 40% on individual objectives set by the VP Sales.

### 7. General Provisions

7.1. Draws: new representatives receive a recoverable draw for up to nine (9) months per their offer letter.

7.2. Disputes must be filed with Sales Operations within sixty (60) days of the commission statement date.

7.3. Nothing in this plan limits the Company's right to reprice, reassign, or reclassify accounts or order channels.

7.4. This plan document is confidential and proprietary to Bluestem Foodservice Distributors, Inc. Distribution outside the Company requires CFO approval.
