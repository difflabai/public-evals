# Task 1: Demand Growth Plan

## Objective

Produce Bluestem's demand growth plan: a segment-level diagnosis of where demand and margin actually come from, a prioritized portfolio of demand-generation initiatives with quantified impact bands grounded in the source data, and a sequencing that respects Bluestem's cash and capability constraints — answering the CEO's question: how do we grow demand without destroying margin?

## Requirements

- Diagnose the segment mix-shift quantitatively: reconcile revenue growth against gross-profit growth FY2023-FY2025 and show which segments create vs consume margin at the contribution level (gross margin less delivery cost-to-serve).
- Reconcile the conflicting churn figures circulating internally and state which definition the plan uses and why.
- Reconcile the conflicting active-account counts and use the correct base.
- Evaluate the VP Sales price-reduction thesis against the Springfield pilot data and the customer survey; state a position with evidence.
- Size the private-label opportunity against the correct peer benchmark cohort.
- Size the street digital-ordering opportunity, and address the sales-force compensation barrier explicitly.
- Address the healthcare whitespace including the Heartland GPO loss post-mortem and what winning the spring 2027 rebid requires operationally.
- Address marketing/food-show ROI measurement.
- Sequence initiatives within the client's capital constraints (~$6M/yr capex, $25M revolver, 3.0x leverage covenant) and DC capacity constraints; state dependencies.
- Follow the Harrow & Vance engagement deliverable standards document (sources/engagement/harrow_vance_engagement_standards.md) in all deliverables.
- Alongside the two deliverables, submit claims.json containing a key_figures object with exactly these self-reported figures from your analysis: net_revenue_attrition_pct, chain_contribution_margin_pct, private_label_penetration_pct (each a percentage on a 0-100 scale, e.g. 8.5 for 8.5%).

## Deliverables

Submit exactly these files:

- **`demand_growth_plan.md`** — the prose demand growth plan.
- **`initiative_portfolio.json`** — the structured initiative portfolio.
- **`claims.json`** — self-reported key figures from your analysis.

### Required schema

initiative_portfolio.json must be valid JSON with these top-level keys: "baseline" — an object including revenue_usd_m (number), gross_margin_pct (number), active_accounts (number), and segments (list of objects each with name, fy2025_revenue_usd_m, gross_margin_pct); "initiatives" — a list of 5-10 objects, each with initiative_id (string), name (string), segment (string), thesis (string), revenue_impact_band_usd_m (two-element list [lo, hi]), margin_impact_bps (number), cost_to_execute_band_usd_k (two-element list [lo, hi]), timeline_quarters (number), dependencies (list of initiative_ids or constraint strings), and evidence_sources (list of source document filenames); "sequencing" — an ordered list describing the rollout phases and their rationale; "risks" — a list of at least 3 risk entries. Also submit claims.json with a key_figures object containing net_revenue_attrition_pct, chain_contribution_margin_pct, and private_label_penetration_pct as numbers.
