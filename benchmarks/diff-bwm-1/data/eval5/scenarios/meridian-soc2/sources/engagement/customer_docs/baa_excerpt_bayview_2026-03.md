# Business Associate Agreement — Bayview Regional Health Network / Meridian Health Loop, Inc.

**Excerpt provided to Ledgerline Assurance for readiness scoping.** Executed for the pilot program; full agreement on file with Bayview Vendor Risk and Meridian legal. Effective Date: 2026-03-01.

"Covered Entity" means Bayview Regional Health Network. "Business Associate" means Meridian Health Loop, Inc. Capitalized terms not otherwise defined have the meanings given in 45 CFR Parts 160 and 164.

---

## §2. Permitted Uses and Disclosures

**2.1** Business Associate may use and disclose PHI solely to perform the services described in the underlying Pilot Services Agreement, as permitted by this BAA, or as Required by Law, and for no other purpose.

**2.2** Business Associate shall not use or disclose PHI in a manner that would violate Subpart E of 45 CFR Part 164 if done by Covered Entity, except as expressly permitted herein.

## §3. Safeguards

**3.1** Business Associate shall implement administrative, physical, and technical safeguards that reasonably and appropriately protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of electronic PHI that it creates, receives, maintains, or transmits on behalf of Covered Entity, in accordance with the HIPAA Security Rule.

**3.2** Business Associate shall designate a security official responsible for the development and implementation of its security policies and procedures.

## §4. Reporting

**4.1** Business Associate shall notify Covered Entity within five (5) business days of Discovery of any Security Incident or Breach of Unsecured PHI. Such notification shall include, to the extent known: the nature of the incident; the PHI involved; the identity of each Individual whose PHI has been, or is reasonably believed to have been, accessed, acquired, used, or disclosed; the steps Individuals should take to protect themselves; and the corrective actions Business Associate has taken or intends to take. Business Associate shall supplement its notification as additional information becomes available.

**4.2** For purposes of §4.1, "Discovery" has the meaning given in 45 CFR §164.410(a)(2): a Breach shall be treated as discovered as of the first day on which such Breach is known to Business Associate or, by exercising reasonable diligence, would have been known to Business Associate, including knowledge imputed from any employee, officer, or agent of Business Associate other than the person committing the Breach.

**4.3** Unsuccessful Security Incidents — including pings, port scans, and unsuccessful log-on attempts that do not result in unauthorized access, use, or disclosure of PHI — are reported in aggregate upon Covered Entity's written request, and this §4.3 constitutes notice thereof.

## §5. Subcontractors

**5.1** Business Associate shall ensure that any Subcontractor that creates, receives, maintains, or transmits PHI on behalf of Business Associate agrees in writing to restrictions and conditions no less restrictive than those that apply to Business Associate under this BAA.

**5.2** Business Associate shall not disclose PHI to, or permit the creation, receipt, maintenance, or transmission of PHI by, any Subcontractor without (a) prior written disclosure of such Subcontractor to Covered Entity, and (b) an executed business associate agreement between Business Associate and such Subcontractor satisfying §5.1. Business Associate shall maintain and furnish to Covered Entity upon request a current list of all such Subcontractors.

## §6. Encryption

**6.1** Business Associate shall encrypt PHI at rest and in transit in accordance with the HIPAA Security Rule and the guidance issued thereunder by the Secretary specifying the technologies and methodologies that render PHI unusable, unreadable, or indecipherable to unauthorized individuals. PHI not so encrypted shall be treated as Unsecured PHI for all purposes of §4.

## §7. Term; Return or Destruction

**7.1** Upon termination of this BAA for any reason, Business Associate shall return or destroy all PHI received from, or created or received on behalf of, Covered Entity, and shall retain no copies, except where return or destruction is infeasible, in which case the protections of this BAA shall extend to such PHI for so long as it is retained.
