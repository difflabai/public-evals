# Master Services Agreement — St. Aldric Health / Meridian Health Loop, Inc.

**Excerpt — §§6–9 only.** Provided to Ledgerline Assurance for readiness scoping purposes. Full agreement on file with Meridian legal (DocuSign envelope MHL-2026-0114). Effective Date: 2026-01-15.

Capitalized terms not defined in this excerpt have the meanings given in §1 (Definitions) of the Agreement. "Provider" means Meridian Health Loop, Inc. "Customer" means St. Aldric Health.

## §6. Data Security

**6.1 Safeguards.** Provider shall implement and maintain administrative, physical, and technical safeguards for the protection of Customer Data that are no less rigorous than accepted industry practices for the protection of protected health information, and shall comply with the Business Associate Agreement executed between the parties, which is incorporated by reference.

**6.2 Personnel.** Provider shall ensure that all Provider personnel with access to Customer Data are bound by written confidentiality obligations and have completed security awareness training appropriate to their role.

**6.3 Notification.** Provider shall notify Customer of any Security Incident affecting Customer Data in accordance with the timelines set forth in the Business Associate Agreement, and shall cooperate with Customer in any resulting investigation or notification effort.

## §7. Service Levels

**7.1 Definitions.** "Monthly Uptime" means the percentage of minutes in a calendar month during which the Platform is available to transmit and receive messages, excluding Scheduled Maintenance windows announced not less than seventy-two (72) hours in advance.

**7.2 Availability Commitment.** Provider will maintain Platform availability of no less than 99.9% Monthly Uptime, measured per §7.3, with service credits per Schedule C.

**7.3 Measurement.** Monthly Uptime shall be measured by Provider's monitoring systems at the Platform API boundary and reported to Customer within ten (10) business days of the close of each calendar month. Customer may dispute any monthly report within thirty (30) days of receipt.

**7.4 Service Credits.** Service credits accrue per Schedule C and are Customer's sole and exclusive remedy for failure to meet the Availability Commitment, except where such failure also constitutes a material breach under §12 (Term and Termination) of the Agreement.

## §8. Confidentiality

**8.1 Customer Data.** Provider shall treat all Customer Data, including without limitation all patient-identifying information and all message content transmitted through the Platform, as Confidential Information of Customer, regardless of whether such data is separately marked confidential.

**8.2 Use Restriction.** Provider shall use Confidential Information solely to perform its obligations under the Agreement and for no other purpose, including without limitation the training of statistical or machine-learning models, absent Customer's prior written consent.

**8.3 Disclosure.** Provider shall not disclose Confidential Information to any third party other than to subcontractors permitted under the Business Associate Agreement, and then only under written obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those in this §8.

**8.4 Return or Destruction.** Upon termination or expiration of the Agreement, Provider shall, at Customer's election, return or destroy all Customer Data in Provider's possession or control within thirty (30) days, and shall certify such destruction in writing, save only such copies as Provider is required by law to retain, which shall remain subject to this §8 for so long as retained.

## §9. Compliance and Audit

**9.1 Regulatory Compliance.** Provider shall perform its obligations in compliance with all applicable federal and state laws and regulations, including HIPAA and the HITECH Act.

**9.2 SOC 2 Report.** Provider shall engage an independent service auditor to perform a SOC 2 Type II examination addressing the security, availability, and confidentiality of the Platform, and shall provide Customer with the resulting report no later than 2027-04-30, and annually thereafter during the Term. Pending delivery of such report, Provider shall complete Customer's security assessment questionnaires within fifteen (15) business days of request.

**9.3 Audit Rights.** No more than once per calendar year, and upon thirty (30) days' written notice, Customer may audit Provider's compliance with §§6–8, either directly or through an independent third party bound by confidentiality obligations, during normal business hours and in a manner that does not unreasonably interfere with Provider's operations.
