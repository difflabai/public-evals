DESCRIPTION (Tom Brzezinski, 2026-03-13 09:41)

While pulling logs to trace the recipient mapping shift from yesterday's
St. Aldric discharge batch (INC-2026-007), I found that workflow-engine
has been running with log level DEBUG in production since the 2.38 deploy
on 2026-01-29. At DEBUG, the send_sms code path logs the full outbound
payload — recipient name, phone number, and the complete message body,
which for the discharge template includes the patient's discharge
instructions.

These logs go to stdout, get picked up by the Datadog agent, and are
indexed in Datadog with 30-day retention. Anyone with a Datadog seat can
query them. Example of the line shape (names and numbers below are from
my local repro seed data, but the prod index has real ones):

  2026-03-12 11:03:47.112 DEBUG [batch-7741] send_sms payload={"recipient_name":"Harold Jenkins","phone":"+1-555-014-2276","template":"er_discharge_followup_v3","body":"Harold, following your visit to the St. Aldric ER on 3/11, please take your prescribed antibiotic twice daily and schedule a follow-up with..."}
  2026-03-12 11:03:47.309 DEBUG [batch-7741] send_sms payload={"recipient_name":"Mabel Sorenson","phone":"+1-555-014-8841","template":"er_discharge_followup_v3","body":"Mabel, following your visit to the St. Aldric ER on 3/11, your care team recommends..."}

Two problems to solve here:
1. Stop the bleeding — log level back to INFO and remove payload logging
   from the send path entirely.
2. Figure out purge/remediation for what is already indexed in Datadog
   (trailing 30-day window, plus whatever landed in the log archive).

Flagging separately that the vendor register has Datadog down as
no-PHI-access, which stopped being true the day 2.38 shipped.
