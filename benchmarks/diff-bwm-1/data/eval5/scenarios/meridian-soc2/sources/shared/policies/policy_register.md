# Meridian Health Loop — Policy Register

**Maintained by:** Alicia Fontaine, Compliance & Privacy Lead
**Register last updated:** 2026-05-15

This register is the index of record for Meridian Health Loop's information security and compliance policies. Each policy has a named owner responsible for its content and for completing the annual review; `next_review_due` is calculated as one year from the last completed review. Approved policy documents live in Notion under Compliance / Policies, with PDF snapshots of each approved version retained for audit purposes.

Owners: please review your policies on or before the due date and send the redlined draft to compliance for approval routing. If a policy needs no changes, an owner sign-off email is sufficient to log the review — but it must be logged, or the register will continue to show the prior review date. Several reviews are currently past due; owners have been notified and the compliance team is tracking closure as part of SOC 2 Type II preparation.
