## Meridian Health Loop Raises $14M Series A Led by Riverline Ventures to Close the Patient Communication Gap

**DURHAM, N.C. — August 19, 2025** — Meridian Health Loop, Inc., the patient communication platform trusted by leading health systems, today announced a $14 million Series A financing led by Riverline Ventures, with participation from existing investors Carolina Seed Collective and Harbor Light Health Capital. The funding will accelerate product development, expand Meridian's clinical operations team, and support the company's growth with health systems nationwide.

Every year, millions of patients leave the emergency department, enroll in clinical studies, or schedule appointments — and then fall out of contact with their care teams. Meridian's platform closes that gap with automated, clinically-reviewed patient workflows delivered over SMS, web, and voice, meeting patients on the channels they already use. Health systems deploying Meridian's ER discharge follow-up workflows have seen meaningful improvements in post-discharge engagement and follow-up completion.

"Patients don't download apps, but they do answer texts," said **Dana Okafor, co-founder and CEO of Meridian Health Loop**. "Health systems spend enormous energy getting care right inside their four walls, and then communication falls off a cliff the moment the patient walks out the door. This funding lets us bring dependable, clinically-sound patient follow-through to many more health systems — and to the patients counting on them."

Founded in 2021 by Dana Okafor and Priya Raman, Meridian Health Loop has grown to more than 50 employees across three states. The company serves academic medical centers and regional health systems, and its platform is built on SOC 2 certified, HIPAA-compliant infrastructure designed for the security expectations of enterprise healthcare.

"We looked at every company in the patient-engagement space, and Meridian stood out for one reason: health systems actually renew and expand," said **Miriam Castellanos, Partner at Riverline Ventures**, who joins Meridian's board of directors. "Dana and Priya have built the rare healthcare product that clinicians trust and patients actually respond to. The engagement data speaks for itself, and we believe Meridian will become the default communication layer between health systems and their patients."

The Series A follows a year of rapid growth in which Meridian expanded its flagship ER discharge follow-up product, launched clinical study enrollment workflows now in use across multiple active studies, and introduced voice-based workflows for patient populations that prefer a phone call to a text message.

With the new capital, Meridian plans to:

- **Expand the clinical operations team**, deepening the clinical review behind every workflow template;
- **Broaden EHR integration coverage**, reducing deployment time for new health-system customers;
- **Invest in analytics**, giving health systems clearer visibility into patient engagement outcomes across service lines; and
- **Grow go-to-market capacity** to meet demand from health systems nationwide.

### About Meridian Health Loop

Meridian Health Loop is a healthcare communications technology company based in Durham, North Carolina. Its platform delivers automated, clinically-reviewed patient workflows — including ER discharge follow-up, clinical study enrollment, and appointment check-in — over SMS, web, and voice on behalf of health systems. Learn more at meridianhealthloop.example.
